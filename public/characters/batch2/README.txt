MIRAI O TSUKURU MONO — DEVELOPMENT BATCH 02
============================================

This ZIP contains a generated visual-development board and separated concept crops
for the main trio, supporting cast groups, SUKA SRI Headquarters, and Part 1–3
location concepts.

IMPORTANT:
- These are concept-board crops, not 93 individually completed high-resolution
  character sheets.
- The generated board visually depicts the requested designs and environments.
- It does not verify every tiny label or all character identities against the
  uploaded 93-character source roster.
- The earlier Phase 1 ZIP contains the 93 numbered source-sketch crops and roster index.
- Full individual colour portraits and true front/side/back turnarounds for all
  93 characters remain future production work.
- The location images are concept art, not a verified scene-by-scene map from
  the Part 1, Part 2 and Part 3 scripts.
