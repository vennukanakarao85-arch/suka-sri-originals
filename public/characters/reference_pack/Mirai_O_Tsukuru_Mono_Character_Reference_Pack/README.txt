IMPORTANT COMPLETENESS NOTE
This is a source-backed starter pack, NOT a verified complete 93-character roster.
The Part 3 story bible explicitly says the complete 93-character roster has not yet
been verified against the original Part 1/Part 2 cast list. Therefore this pack does
not invent missing names or claim that all 93 characters are present.

Canon/proposal distinction:
- The PDF marks Zero Archivist, Aoi Kisaragi, Rei Kurogane and Mei Arisawa as optional
  proposed characters, not established canon.
- Maki-zushi (巻き寿司), creator of VGS, is included because the user approved her
  in the conversation. No official portrait for her was present in the two supplied
  gallery boards.
- Family/council and Syndicate group portraits do not provide individual names.

To produce the exact full 93-character edition, the original Part 1/Part 2 character
roster or the relevant reference PDF/pages are still needed.
